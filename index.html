BudTune

Soundcore earbuds on Windows

Manage compatible Soundcore earbuds over Bluetooth. Check battery levels, change EQ, and adjust noise control or gestures where your model supports them.

Get BudTune on SourceForge

Which earbuds can I use?

BudTune includes profiles for Liberty 5, Liberty 4 Pro, Liberty 4 NC, P31i, P20i, R50i and Sport X20. Available settings vary by model and firmware. Liberty 5 Pro, K20i, AeroClip 2 and AeroFit Pro are pending device support. The Windows release is a preview.

Connect your earbuds

  1. Pair your earbuds in Windows Bluetooth settings.
  2. Download and open the portable BudTune.exe for Windows x64.
  3. Add a device, select the exact model, then choose the paired earbuds.

Frequently asked questions

Is BudTune free?
Yes. BudTune is free, open-source software under GPL-3.0-or-later.
Can I control ANC on my R50i?
The R50i (A3949) profile provides EQ and supported device controls. R50i and R50i NC are different models. Noise cancellation controls depend on your model.
Where do I find new versions?
See GitHub release notes for changes and checksums, or download through SourceForge.
